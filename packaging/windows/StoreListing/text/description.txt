WinNewsReader is a free feed reader for Windows 10 and 11, a native port of NetNewsWire for the Mac. Follow the sites you care about and read them in a clean three-pane window, with no account required.

Reads RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and RSS-in-JSON. Keep your feeds on your PC, or sync them with FreshRSS, Feedbin, NewsBlur, The Old Reader or BazQux.

Use NetNewsWire's article themes, search your articles, star what you want to keep, undo mistakes with Ctrl+Z, and see your unread count on the taskbar. A column layout turns the timeline into a table you can sort, and a status bar keeps the counts and theme close at hand.

WinNewsReader is open source under the MIT license. It is a port of NetNewsWire by Brent Simmons and contributors, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by NetNewsWire.
