Your news, on your PC.

WinNewsReader is a native feed reader for Windows 10 and 11. Follow the sites you care about through RSS, Atom and JSON Feed, and read them in a clean three-pane window. It’s built in the spirit of NetNewsWire, the much-loved Mac reader.

Download forWindows

The installer is code-signed, but Windows may still warn until it builds a reputation.

What it does today

  • Every common feed format

    RSS, RSS 1.0, Atom, JSON Feed and RSS-in-JSON, using NetNewsWire’s parsers ported to C#, so the same feed reads the same way.

  • Finds the feed for you

    Type a site’s address and it finds the site’s feed, the way NetNewsWire does.

  • Folders and OPML

    Organise feeds into folders, and bring your subscriptions in or take them out as OPML.

  • Smart feeds

    Today, All Unread and Starred gather articles from every feed. Hide what you’ve read, per feed or across the sidebar.

  • Made for the keyboard

    NetNewsWire’s shortcuts, with Ctrl for Cmd: Space to page through and on to the next unread, n, s, k and the rest.

  • At home on Windows

    A native WinUI 3 app with Mica, dark mode and resizable panes. Your feeds stay on your PC.

  • Sync with your service

    Keep your feeds, folders and read and starred articles in step with Feedbin, NewsBlur, The Old Reader, BazQux or FreshRSS.

On the way

  • Sync with Inoreader and Feedly, which need API access first
  • Notifications, preferences and the rest of NetNewsWire’s features
  • A signed installer with automatic updates

WinNewsReader is a Windows port of NetNewsWire by Brent Simmons and contributors, used under the MIT license. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by NetNewsWire.
