WinNewsReader 1.0.0

2026-10-30

The first release: a native Windows port of NetNewsWire.

  • Reads RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and RSS-in-JSON, with NetNewsWire's article themes.
  • Syncs with FreshRSS, Feedbin, NewsBlur, The Old Reader and BazQux (File → Add Account…), or keeps your feeds on your PC.
  • Reading: Find in Article (Ctrl+F), undo for marking read or starred and deleting a feed or folder, a taskbar unread badge, and feed site icons.
  • Layout: a mail-style column layout with the table across the top, sorting by feed, title, unread and starred, Group by Feed, and a search scope button.
  • Status bar: a selection menu, a theme menu and click-to-cycle counts.
  • Windows: Current Activity, Activity Log, Error Log, Account Stats and Dinosaurs (Ctrl+6 to Ctrl+9).
  • Subscribe by dragging links onto the sidebar.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns when you install.

WinNewsReader is a Windows port of NetNewsWire by Brent Simmons and contributors, used under the MIT license. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by NetNewsWire.
