Feedbin, NewsBlur, The Old Reader and BazQux, and a mail-style column layout

2026-10-29

Sync with Feedbin, NewsBlur, The Old Reader and BazQux, as well as FreshRSS: File → Add Account… lists the services and asks for your sign-in. The column layout now puts the table across the top with the article below it, both full width, like a mail reader. In the status bar the gear has an arrow, and the theme menu moves to the right with the theme's name.

Utility windows, a column layout and a status bar

2026-10-29

Current Activity, Activity Log, Error Log, Account Stats and Dinosaurs windows (Ctrl+6 to Ctrl+9); a table-style column layout with sorting by feed, title, unread and starred; Group by Feed; a search scope button; a status bar with a selection menu, a theme menu and click-to-cycle counts; and dragging links onto the sidebar to subscribe.

Find in Article, undo, and a taskbar badge

2026-10-28

Ctrl+F finds text in the article you're reading, Ctrl+Z undoes marking read or starred and deleting a feed or folder, the taskbar icon shows your unread count, and feeds show their sites' icons.

WinNewsReader is in development

2026-10-24

WinNewsReader is a Windows port of NetNewsWire that reads RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and RSS-in-JSON, keeps your feeds on your PC or syncs with FreshRSS, and uses NetNewsWire's article themes. It isn't released yet; the source is on GitHub and an installer is still to come.

WinNewsReader is a Windows port of NetNewsWire by Brent Simmons and contributors, used under the MIT license. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by NetNewsWire.
